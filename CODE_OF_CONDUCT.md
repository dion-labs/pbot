# Code of conduct

pbot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, keep technical disagreement focused on the work, and protect the privacy of anyone whose device or account evidence appears in a report. Do not post credentials, account details, device identifiers, private screenshots, local paths, or files from `var/`.

Report conduct concerns privately through the Dion Labs organization on GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
